Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Repair Dashboard, an internal tool of the Computechsale laptop repair team. Last updated 28 October 2026.

What the dashboard reads from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, the dashboard receives only your Google account's email address and the confirmation that it is verified. It uses the address to match you to your name on the team's list. It does not read your contacts, calendar, Drive files, or anything else in your Google account.

What is stored

The team list (name and email address of each team member) is kept by the shop owner in the dashboard's own settings. A record of each sign-in (time and email address) is kept in the owner's private spreadsheet to keep the tool secure. The work you record in the dashboard (repairs, parts, inventory) is saved in the team's own Google Sheets, as it was before the dashboard existed.

What is shared

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside the Computechsale team. No advertising, no analytics services.

Removing your access

Ask the shop owner to take your email address off the team list. You can also remove the dashboard from your Google account at any time under Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
