Repair Dashboard

An internal tool for the Computechsale laptop repair team.

The Repair Dashboard is where the repair technicians record their work during the day: which laptops they repaired, which parts they requested and what is in the parts inventory. It is used only by the team and is not available to the public.

Technicians sign in with their Google account. The dashboard reads only the account's email address, to know which team member is at the keyboard. See the privacy policy.
